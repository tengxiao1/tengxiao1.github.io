Teng Xiao

I work at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2) and the University of Washington with Prof. Noah A. Smith and Prof. Hanna Hajishirzi. I completed my PhD at Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. Vasant Honavar.

I am interested in machine learning and reinforcement learning. I am currently working on: (i) Alignment and Reasoning for LLM Agents; (ii) Long-horizon LLM Agent for Open-ended Tasks.
